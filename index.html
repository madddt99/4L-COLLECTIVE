4L

TAQI (feat. ZIEN4L) // 2023

“4L” by TAQI (feat. ZIEN4L) is a statement of loyalty, ambition, and identity. The title, “4L” (“For Life”), represents an unbreakable commitment to the crew and the lifestyle they’ve built together. The song blends confident verses about chasing success, overcoming obstacles, and staying true to the people who have been there from the start. It carries a determined, motivational tone while making it clear that loyalty comes before everything else.

In short, the song is about:

  • Loyalty to the team and the “4L” family.
  • Staying committed through success and hardship.
  • Building a legacy through hard work and ambition.
  • Moving forward together while ignoring doubt and negativity.

SYSTEM

ZIEN4L // Debut Album // 2024

SYSTEM is the highly anticipated debut album by ZIEN4L. Moving away from the collective umbrella to establish his own distinct artistic universe, ZIEN4L pieces together intense vocal performances with intricate, hard-hitting production. It's a dark, experimental, and undeniable standalone project.
