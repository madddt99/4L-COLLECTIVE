4L

4L PROJECT | COLLECTIVE is an Egyptian creative collective redefining the underground through music, visuals, and culture. Home to ZIEN, TAQI, NMS, 4L exists to create without limits—blending trap, rage, and experimental hip-hop into a distinct artistic universe. Every release is part of a larger vision, driven by collaboration, originality, and the pursuit of a lasting legacy.
